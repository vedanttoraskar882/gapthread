Platform Capabilities

One Performance Thread. Multiple Operational Domains.

GapThread brings performance governance together without forcing operators to replace the specialist tools they already trust.

01

Multi-Source KPI Ingestion Hub

Connects labour, stock, compliance, EPOS and guest-experience systems through documented APIs or scheduled exports and normalises incoming information into a common KPI structure.

02

Variance & Exception Detection

Flags KPI movements that breach configurable thresholds at the site and KPI level so managers can focus on meaningful exceptions rather than dashboard noise.

03

Root-Cause Capture Workspace

Provides a structured place for managers to record what happened, the assessed cause and supporting evidence such as rota extracts, photographs, invoices or other operational records.

04

Action-Plan Governance Workflow

Turns an agreed response into an accountable action with a named owner, deadline, resourcing requirements and approval where required.

05

Next-Cycle Verification Engine

Re-checks the same KPI during the next review cycle and records whether the action was Resolved, Partially Resolved, or Recurred.

06

Cross-Site Escalation & Portfolio Dashboard

Provides area and regional managers with a ranked view of open, overdue and recurring operational actions across the sites they oversee.

Operating Rhythm

From Raw KPI Data to Verified Operational Improvement

A continuous seven-step loop that transforms fragmented review meetings into governed, verifiable outcomes.

PULL → FLAG → DIAGNOSE → PLAN → VERIFY
Resolved Partially Resolved Recurred
01 CONNECT

Connect Existing Systems

GapThread connects to the operational systems already used for labour, stock, compliance, EPOS and guest experience through supported APIs or scheduled exports.

02 PULL

Build the Review Snapshot

Relevant site KPIs are brought into a common structure for each review period, creating a consistent baseline across domains.

03 FLAG

Surface Meaningful Variances

Configurable thresholds highlight the KPIs that genuinely require management attention, filtering out routine fluctuations.

04 DIAGNOSE

Understand the Root Cause

Managers record what happened, why it happened and the evidence behind the diagnosis. Precedent and guest-sentiment context can support the investigation.

05 PLAN

Turn Decisions Into Accountable Actions

Every agreed action receives an owner, deadline and required resourcing so corrective work is no longer lost inside meeting notes.

06 VERIFY

Check the Same KPI Again

At the next review cycle, GapThread re-checks the KPI associated with the action and records whether the issue has been resolved.

07 LEARN

Build Operational Memory

Verified results become searchable organisational knowledge that helps teams understand what has worked before across the estate.

Enterprise Trust

Governance Built Into the Architecture

Engineered specifically for multi-tenant hospitality estates with clear boundaries and auditable operations.

Tenant-Level Isolation

Operator data is designed to remain isolated between tenants across data ingestion, storage and review workspaces.

Role-Based Access

Access to diagnoses, actions and approvals can be controlled according to user responsibility from site manager to regional lead.

Immutable Audit Trail

Diagnosis, action and verification events are recorded for stronger operational governance, labour compliance and auditability.

Anonymised Benchmarking

Where cross-operator data is used for benchmarking, it is designed to be anonymised so one operator’s performance data is not directly visible to another.
